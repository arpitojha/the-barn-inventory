Local-first by design

No Microsoft account, licence, or Copilot dependency. The app runs offline after first load and stores everything in this browser. Inventory rules live in src/core.mjs and storage is isolated behind an adapter in src/storage.mjs, so a shared multi-device backend can be added later without touching the screens.
